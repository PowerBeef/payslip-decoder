# Payslip Decoder

Open your Workday payslip PDF and get every line explained in plain English or French, with independent checks of the math, the federal and Québec deductions, and the yearly maximums.

**Ask questions.** A small question model, trained for payslips and built into the page, answers questions in French or English about your pay, your time off, the yearly maximums, tax slips and payroll terms, and explains what changed between two payslips. It runs on your device like the rest of the app.

**Private by design.** The PDF is read by your browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, stored on a server or sent anywhere.

**Install it like an app.** On iPhone or iPad: Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android: the browser menu, then Install app or Add to home screen. It then opens full screen and works offline.

Independent tool. Not affiliated with any employer, Workday or any union. It explains your payslip; it doesn't replace it.

Uses [PDF.js](https://mozilla.github.io/pdf.js/) (Apache 2.0, see `vendor/`) and the Barlow typeface (SIL Open Font License).
