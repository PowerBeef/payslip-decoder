# Payslip Decoder

Open your Workday payslip PDF and get every line explained in plain English or French, with independent checks of the math, the federal and Québec deductions, and the yearly maximums.

**Who it's for.** People in Québec who are paid through Workday and want to understand their payslip: what each line means, where the money went, and whether the numbers add up. Each payslip is checked with the government figures of its own year.

**Ask questions.** A small question model, trained for payslips and built into the page, answers questions in French or English about your pay, your time off, the yearly maximums, tax slips and payroll terms, and explains what changed between two payslips. It runs on your device like the rest of the app.

**Private by design.** The PDF is read by your browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, stored on a server or sent anywhere, and the page's security policy stops it from contacting any other site.

**Install it like an app.** On iPhone or iPad: Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android: the browser menu, then Install app or Add to home screen. It then opens full screen and works offline.

## Browsers

The app needs a browser recent enough for the PDF reader it uses (PDF.js), which supports Safari 18 or newer (iPhone and iPad on iOS 18 or newer) and Chrome or Edge 125 or newer. Older versions may still work but aren't supported.

## Something looks wrong?

Open the Checks view (at the end of Pay) and tap **Copy a report**, then send it to the person who shared the app with you. The report lists which checks passed or failed and the line names the app didn't recognize. It holds no amount, name, date or account number.
